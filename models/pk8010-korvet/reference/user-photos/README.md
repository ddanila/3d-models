- Shallow concavity visible across the top; exact curvature is not established.
- Front and rear profiles appear asymmetric in the rotated top view.
- Rectangular mounting tube inside the shell, with internal supporting webs.
- White filled triangular up-arrow and numeral 8 on the top face.

Perspective and the different distances of the ruler, top face, and socket
from the camera prevent reliable dimensions from a single image scale.
Overall height, socket insertion depth, socket wall thickness, shell thickness,
and exact top dimensions remain unmeasured. A side-on height measurement and
the depth of the rectangular socket are the next useful measurements.
