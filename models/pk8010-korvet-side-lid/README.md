In top view, with the origin at the base's bottom-left corner, the hole
center is at X = 131 mm, Y = 10 mm. It passes through the full 4 mm thickness.

An oval finger recess shares the hole center. It slopes gradually from the
25 × 17 mm outline at the top surface to the circular hole rim 1 mm below.
These approximate dimensions give 7.5 mm around the hole horizontally and
3.5 mm vertically. The oval reaches Y = 1.5 mm, slightly beyond the raised
section's bottom edge at Y = 2 mm, so the recess opens slightly onto that
edge. The lower base ledge remains intact.

Both decorative groove segments run horizontally at Y = 10 mm and cut only
the raised section, from Z = 4 down to Z = 3 mm. The right segment runs
inward from the raised section's right edge (X = 152) to X = 145 mm,
leaving a 9 mm gap to the hole. The left segment runs 113 mm inward from
the raised section's left edge (X = 2) to X = 115 mm, leaving an 11 mm gap
to the hole. Both lengths count only the raised section.

The transition is a square step. Print with the broad base on the bed,
as modeled; no supports are needed. Dimensions are editable in `model.scad`.
